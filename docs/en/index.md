features:
  - icon: 📚
    title: CBZ, CBR and PDF
    details: Zip, RAR 4 and 5 (solid included) and PDF read without conversion or full extraction; JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, BMP and GIF pages. For a PDF, the artwork is shown without the page margins.
  - icon: 📖
    title: A reader for every format
    details: Right-to-left manga, comics, double pages, continuous webtoon scrolling. Progress follows you from one device to another.
  - icon: 🛡️
    title: Checked integrity
    details: Every page is checked when scanned. A damaged page shows an explicit error, never another image. The health of every library is visible at a glance.
  - icon: 👀
    title: Watched library
    details: A file added or removed is scanned on its own. A periodic full scan catches up with network shares.
  - icon: 📱
    title: Installable app
    details: PWA for tablets and phones, downloaded volumes for offline reading, pages resized to the screen in AVIF.
  - icon: 👨‍👩‍👧
    title: Accounts and security
    details: One account per person, libraries opened per account, two-factor authentication, API tokens, automatic backups.
  - icon: 🏷️
    title: Metadata
    details: ComicInfo.xml, index.json and PDF properties, completed on request by AniList and MangaDex. Your own edits always come first.
  - icon: 🏠
    title: Self-hosted
    details: A single Rust binary (Linux, Windows, macOS) or a Docker image. Your files are never modified and nothing leaves your network without your consent.
